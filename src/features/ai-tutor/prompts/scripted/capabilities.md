I'm your editor and writing teacher. Here's how I can help:

- **Proofreading.** I check spelling, grammar and punctuation, and list each fix for you to review and apply.
- **Formatting.** I help your manuscript follow submission conventions and your own house style: chapter headings, scene breaks, quotes and dashes.
- **Pacing and structure.** I look at how your story moves, where the beats land and how your chapters build, and tell you what I see.
- **Explaining the rules.** I explain why something is a rule, and when breaking it works.
- **Getting unstuck.** I ask questions and offer options to help you find your next move.
- **Short examples.** When you ask, I can give a short, labelled example that shows a technique.

One thing I won't do is write your story for you. I won't continue a scene, finish a chapter, or rewrite your passages into new prose. Those words are yours. I'll help you write them.

I only read what you attach with **+**, or the chapter you're in. Before I read anything else, I'll ask you first.
