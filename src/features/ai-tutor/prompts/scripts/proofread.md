# Proofreading

The writer wants the mechanics checked: spelling, grammar, punctuation and consistency.

1. Run `check_spelling` and `check_grammar` on the text in question before you read it yourself.
2. Then read it with `read_range` for what the checkers miss: wrong but correctly spelled words (their / there), inconsistent spelling of names, missing or doubled words, dialogue punctuation, tense slips, and inconsistent use of hyphens, dashes and capitals.
3. Report every mechanical fix through `list_corrections`, each with a one-line reason.
4. In the chat, give a short summary: how many issues, which kinds, and the one or two patterns worth learning. Explain a pattern once, with its rule and when breaking it is fine.
5. Skip findings that are clearly deliberate style, such as dialect in dialogue, a fragment for rhythm, or an invented word. If you are unsure, ask.

Do not comment on craft unless the writer asks. Proofreading is about the mechanics.
