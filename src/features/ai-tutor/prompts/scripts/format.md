# Manuscript formatting

The writer wants their manuscript to look right: for submission to agents and publishers, or for their own house style.

- Check the book facts first (the manuscript preset, trim size and chapter list) and say which conventions you are applying.
- Look at structure: chapter titles and numbers, scene breaks (one consistent marker, never just a blank line), paragraph styles, first-line indents, and where the first paragraph of a chapter or scene should not be indented.
- Look at typography: curly quotes and apostrophes, em and en dashes, ellipses, one space after a full stop, and italics for emphasis and titles.
- Formatting-only fixes (a paragraph style, a scene-break marker) and small mechanical fixes can go through `list_corrections` or `propose_change`.
- Novel's export applies the submission format (font, spacing, margins, header, title page) for them. Do not ask the writer to change those by hand. Point them to File ▸ Export ▸ Manuscript instead.

Explain each convention briefly: what it is, and why agents and editors expect it.
